# Ads in AI assistants and answer engines

A new category, moving quickly. Verify availability in the business's market before building an idea on it, and keep the evidence label honest: several facts below come only from trade press because the official pages could not be read.

## How it works
- People describe problems in full sentences and ask follow-up questions, so the context of an ad is far richer than a keyword. Ads appear alongside or within answers, matched by the assistant's system to the conversation's topic.
- Advertisers usually supply campaigns and assets; the platform decides placement and often generates or adapts the wording.

## Google: AI Overviews and AI Mode
Reached through existing Google Ads campaigns using AI-powered matching (broad match, AI Max, Performance Max), not a separate buy. Ads in AI Mode: UK planned for Q4 2026, not yet live (Google, 28 October 2026). New AI Mode formats in testing. Full detail in `google-search-and-shopping.md`.

## Microsoft Copilot
Ads in Copilot come from Microsoft Advertising campaigns and assets. Microsoft has also talked publicly about Copilot checkout and brand agents. *Reported and from Microsoft blog headlines; details not verified.* See `microsoft-advertising.md`.

## ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- **Verified at source** (OpenAI, "New ways to buy ChatGPT ads", 5 May 2026): ads began as a pilot bought on cost per thousand impressions; cost per click added on 5 May 2026; a self-serve Ads Manager beta rolling out gradually; buying also through agencies (Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis, WPP) and technology partners (Adobe, Criteo, Kargo, Pacvue, StackAdapt); conversion tracking through a pixel and Conversions API; advertisers see aggregated results, not conversations; OpenAI's system controls delivery.
- **Official announcements exist but could not be read**: OpenAI posts titled "ChatGPT Ads expands across Europe" and "ChatGPT Ads expands to 26 new countries". Read them directly before quoting markets.
- **Reported (trade press)**: ads shown only to logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans, not Plus, Pro, Team or Enterprise; UK ads live from 6 June 2026 and UK self-serve from 22 June 2026, with no minimum spend; personalised ads in the EU require opt-in.
- **Fit question**: if the target buyers are professionals on paid plans, they may never see the ads.

## Perplexity
Stopped taking new advertisers in 2025 and withdrew ads in early 2026. *Reported (Financial Times via trade press).* Not buyable; appearing in its answers is an organic (non-paid) matter.

## Claude and Gemini app
Claude carries no ads. No confirmed ad launch in the Gemini app at the last check.

## Best for and weak for
- **Best for**: considered purchases researched through questions (financial products, software, travel, home improvement, health-adjacent services where policy allows); early learning while competition is thin.
- **Weak for**: impulse products; audiences on ad-free paid plans; advertisers without solid conversion tracking (results will be hard to read).
