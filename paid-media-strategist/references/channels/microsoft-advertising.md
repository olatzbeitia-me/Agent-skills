# Microsoft Advertising

## How it works
- Search auctions on Bing, and on partner search engines that use Microsoft's ads (including Yahoo and DuckDuckGo), with ranking logic similar to Google's (bid combined with quality and relevance).
- **Microsoft Audience Network**: native and display ads across MSN, Outlook, Microsoft Edge and partner sites.
- **Copilot**: ads can appear in Microsoft's Copilot assistant, drawn from existing Microsoft Advertising campaigns and assets.
- Campaign types mirror Google's: Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Audience ads, and AI Max for Search.

## Targeting
Keywords and match types; locations, schedule and devices; remarketing and customer lists; in-market audiences; and **LinkedIn profile targeting** (company, industry, job function and, since 2026, job seniority) on Search and Audience ads. That last one has no equivalent on Google and makes Microsoft a strong option for business-to-business search.

## Bidding and optimisation
Smart bidding equivalents (maximise conversions, target cost per acquisition, target return on ad spend, maximise clicks), plus manual and enhanced cost per click.

## Measurement
Universal Event Tracking tag; enhanced conversions; offline conversion import; consent mode; Performance Max uplift experiments.

## Best for and weak for
- **Best for**: business-to-business (LinkedIn targeting, workplace desktop use); older and higher-income consumer audiences; advertisers already succeeding on Google who want more of the same intent at often lower competition; easy to start by importing Google campaigns.
- **Weak for**: very young audiences; categories with little Bing search volume in the market concerned.
- **Watch for**: Google imports carrying over settings that don't suit Microsoft; Audience Network placements needing review.

## Creative needs
Low; text, images and feeds largely reused from Google.

## Recent developments (checked October 2026)
- **AI Max for Search generally available** (August 2026). *Source: Microsoft Advertising blog, August 2026 product news.*
- **LinkedIn job seniority targeting** (ten levels from chief executive to entry) generally available for Audience ads and Search. *Same source.*
- **Performance Max**: negative keywords (March 2026, lists up to 5,000, Search and Shopping inventory only); age and gender targeting generally available from 8 September 2026; uplift experiments to measure what Performance Max adds alongside other campaigns; search terms in the standard search term report; Ad Preview Hub. *Source: Microsoft Advertising blog, March and August 2026.*
- **Maximum cost per click cap** no longer available on new non-portfolio maximise campaigns from 1 October 2026. *Same source.*
- **Microsoft Advertising MCP server** (read-only, beta) connecting account data to AI assistants for reporting and research. *Same source.*
- Announced at Activate 2026 (June): customer acquisition goals in Performance Max, content targeting on MSN and Outlook, impression-based remarketing, AI-generated creative with brand controls. Availability not stated. *Source: Microsoft Advertising blog.*
