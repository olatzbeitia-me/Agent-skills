# Programmatic, connected TV and video on demand, digital out-of-home

## How programmatic works
- Advertising space on websites, apps, streaming services and digital screens is sold impression by impression through exchanges (supply-side platforms) and bought through **demand-side platforms** (Display & Video 360, The Trade Desk, Amazon DSP, StackAdapt and others).
- Deal types: open exchange (cheapest, least control), private marketplaces (selected publishers, agreed floor prices), programmatic guaranteed (fixed volume and price).
- Targeting: contextual (page or programme content), audience data (third-party, retailer, publisher), the advertiser's own customer data, location and device.
- Costs include media plus platform, data, verification and agency fees; the share that reaches actual media can be well below the headline spend.

## Connected TV and broadcaster video on demand (UK)
- **Broadcasters**: ITVX (bought through ITV, including its Planet V programmatic platform), Channel 4 streaming, Sky (including Sky AdSmart, addressable advertising across Sky homes), Channel 5.
- **Streamers**: Netflix and Amazon Prime Video carry ads in the UK; YouTube on television screens.
- **Self-serve TV**: Comcast's **Universal Ads** launched in the UK on **23 June 2026** with ITV, Channel 4 and Sky, aimed at small and medium-sized businesses and advertisers new to TV: set a budget, choose an audience, upload creative and launch; a Universal Ads pixel links TV exposure to site visits and sales; independent verification through Lantern (the broadcasters' measurement tool) due in 2027. *Source: Channel 4 press release, 23 June 2026.* Agency commentary describes the inventory as video on demand rather than linear TV; the release doesn't specify, so check.
- **LinkedIn connected TV**: see `linkedin.md`; UK availability unconfirmed.

## Digital out-of-home
Digital screens (roadside, rail, Underground, malls) bought by time, location and audience, increasingly programmatically. Useful for local or London-centric presence and for amplifying launches.

## Measurement
View-through windows inflate programmatic results more than anywhere else. Use geographic lift tests, brand search lift, brand lift studies and, for TV, matched-market tests. Verification partners for viewability, fraud and brand safety.

## Best for and weak for
- **Programmatic best for**: reach at scale with the advertiser's own data or contextual targeting; retargeting; specialist audiences through curated deals. **Weak for**: small budgets on open exchange (waste on made-for-advertising sites and apps).
- **TV and video on demand best for**: building fame and trust; mass-market consumer brands; business-to-business brands with large buying groups; categories where being "as seen on TV" adds credibility. **Weak for**: advertisers without broadcast-quality video; businesses needing immediate, measurable leads at small budgets.
