# TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, Reddit and X

## TikTok

**How it works.** An auction for placements in the For You feed, Search, TopView (first ad on opening the app) and Pulse (alongside top content). Content is served by interest rather than by who people follow, so a good ad can reach far beyond any defined audience. Creative that looks and sounds native (creator-style, sound on, a hook in the first second or two) decides results.

**Campaign types.** Objectives from reach to sales. Smart+ (automated targeting, bidding and creative); Spark Ads (boosting organic or creator posts); lead generation with instant forms; catalogue ads; TikTok Shop with GMV Max (optimising for shop revenue); Search Ads.

**Best for:** consumer brands with products that can be shown; audiences under about 45; launches and trend moments; ecommerce through TikTok Shop. **Weak for:** most business-to-business; regulated products that are hard to show natively; advertisers who can't produce frequent native video.

**Recent developments (checked October 2026).** Q3 2026 product preview: TopReach Max Reach (TopView and TopFeed in one buy; allowlist, select regions); TopView self-serve in Ads Manager (select markets, allowlist); Smart+ Search Ads; auto-selection of best assets for lead generation; Google Analytics signals in sales campaigns; Pulse Mentions (ads next to organic content about a brand or competitors; US and Canada); Symphony AI creative tools; an Agentic Hub including an Ads Manager MCP interface. UK availability mostly not stated. *Source: TikTok for Business blog, Q3 2026 product preview.*

**Where to check:** TikTok for Business blog (ads.tiktok.com/business/en-GB/blog), TikTok Newsroom, TikTok Business Help Centre.

## Pinterest
**How it works.** A visual discovery and planning platform; people save ideas for future purchases. Search and browse behaviour signals intent weeks or months ahead. **Best for:** home and interiors, fashion, beauty, food, weddings, travel, DIY; long planning cycles. **Weak for:** business-to-business; impulse or urgent purchases. **Check:** Pinterest Newsroom and Pinterest Business blog.

## Snapchat
**How it works.** Camera-first messaging with Stories, Spotlight and Discover; augmented reality lenses as an ad format. **Best for:** reaching UK teenagers and young adults; youth-skewed consumer brands; launches. **Weak for:** older audiences; business-to-business. **Check:** Snap for Business newsroom.

## Reddit
**How it works.** Community-based: target by subreddit (community), interest, keywords in conversations. Users research purchases and are sceptical of obvious advertising; conversation ads and promoted posts need to fit community norms. **Best for:** technology, gaming, personal finance, niche hobbies, business-to-business software researched in forums; testing messaging with sceptical buyers. **Weak for:** mass-market products needing scale; brands that can't handle candid comments. **Check:** Reddit for Business blog.

## X
**How it works.** Real-time conversation; reach at relatively low cost for news, sport, technology and finance audiences. **Watch for:** brand safety and suitability; check controls available. **Best for:** live moments and events in those categories. **Check:** X Business blog.

For Pinterest, Snapchat, Reddit and X, no 2026 developments have been verified for this skill. Check each newsroom before relying on a feature.
