# Retail media and audio

## Retail media
**How it works.** Retailers sell advertising on their own websites, apps and in stores, and increasingly off-site using their shopper data. The attraction is closed-loop measurement: the retailer can see whether exposed shoppers bought.

**Main UK options.** Amazon Ads (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Amazon DSP, Prime Video ads); Tesco's media and insight platform (dunnhumby data); Nectar360 (Sainsbury's and Argos); Boots Media Group; other grocery, pharmacy and marketplace networks.

**Best for:** brands whose products are sold through that retailer; consumer packaged goods; launches needing shelf and search visibility. **Weak for:** products not sold through the retailer; business-to-business; services.

**Watch for:** sales that would have happened anyway (ask for incrementality measurement); minimum commitments; data access terms.

**Where to check:** Amazon Ads blog; each retailer's media business press pages; IAB UK retail media guidance.

## Audio and podcasts
**How it works.** Ads in streaming music (Spotify free tier), digital radio (Global's DAX, Bauer), and podcasts, either inserted programmatically or read by the host.

**Best for:** brand-building; reaching people during commutes and work; niche business-to-business and specialist audiences through targeted podcasts (host-read ads carry the host's credibility). **Weak for:** products that need to be seen; immediate response without a memorable call to action.

**Measurement:** promotional codes, memorable web addresses, branded search lift, post-purchase "how did you hear about us" surveys, brand lift studies.

**Where to check:** Spotify Advertising news; Global and Bauer advertising sites; Acast; Radiocentre (UK commercial audio body) research.

No 2026 developments have been verified for this skill in these channels. Check the sources above before relying on any feature.
