# Google Performance Max, YouTube and Demand Gen

## How they work
- **Performance Max**: one campaign that buys across all Google inventory (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail, Maps) towards a single conversion goal. The advertiser supplies goals, budget, assets (text, images, video), a product feed if retail, and audience *signals* (hints, not limits). Google's system decides where and to whom to show ads.
- **YouTube**: video auctions across in-stream (skippable and non-skippable), in-feed, Shorts and YouTube on television screens. Bought through Video, Demand Gen or Performance Max campaigns, or programmatically through Display & Video 360.
- **Demand Gen**: visual and video ads across YouTube (including Shorts), Discover and Gmail, built to create demand among people not yet searching. Supports lookalike segments built from the advertiser's own lists.

## Targeting
- Performance Max: audience signals, customer lists, search themes, brand exclusions, campaign-level negative keywords, URL inclusions and exclusions, customer acquisition goals, age and device controls.
- YouTube and Demand Gen: customer lists, lookalikes, custom segments (built from search terms, websites or apps people use), in-market, life events, topics, placements.

## Bidding and optimisation
Performance Max uses Smart Bidding towards conversions or value. Video campaigns can buy on views, reach (cost per thousand impressions) or conversions. Demand Gen uses conversion or click-based bidding.

## Measurement
Same tagging as Search. Video and Demand Gen value is better judged with brand lift studies, conversion lift studies or geographic tests than with last-click reports. Report view-through conversions separately and don't let them carry decisions alone.

## Best for and weak for
- **Performance Max best for**: ecommerce with a strong feed and enough conversion volume; advertisers who can supply video and image assets; businesses with a reliable value signal.
- **Performance Max weak for**: lead generation without qualified-lead tracking (Display and partner inventory can produce low-quality leads); small budgets split across too many campaigns; brands that need strict control over where ads appear.
- **YouTube and Demand Gen best for**: building awareness and consideration; products that need showing; reaching audiences before they search.

## Creative needs
Medium to high. Performance Max without video may auto-generate it, usually poorly. YouTube needs the brand and message in the first five seconds and vertical cuts for Shorts.

## UK notes
YouTube reaches the large majority of UK adults; Ofcom's annual Media Nations and Online Nation reports are the official reference for viewing habits by age.

## Recent developments (checked October 2026)
- **Performance Max controls and reporting**: channel performance reporting, search terms reporting and campaign-level negative keywords (introduced 2025), followed in 2026 by first-party audience exclusions, a budget report projecting month-end spend, audience reporting by age and gender, and placement reports segmented by network. *Source: Google, "New Performance Max steering and reporting updates coming in 2026".* Negative keywords in Performance Max only apply to Search and Shopping inventory.
- **Smart Bidding Exploration** extending to Performance Max with product feeds (beta announced May 2026). *Source: Google Ads & Commerce Blog.*
