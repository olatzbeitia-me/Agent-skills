# Pipeline forecast: Q4 2026 (October to December)

**Target:** £5,600,000  
**Base forecast:** £5,075,084 (91% of target, short of target)  
**Gap to close:** £524,916

Pipeline created so far this quarter (2026-10): £1,640,500. Months still to come: 2026-11, 2026-12, with 3,900 leads planned.

## What it would take to close the gap

Each row is one lever on its own, with everything else at the base rate. "Realistic" means the level needed has been reached in at least one recent month.

| Lever | Now | Needed | Best recent month | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| More leads in the remaining months | 3,900 total | 4,496 total (2,248 a month) (+15%) | 2,300 in a month | Realistic |
| Better lead to marketing qualified lead rate | 34.0% | 39.2% (+15%) | 35.0% | Beyond recent performance |
| Better marketing qualified to sales qualified rate | 36.5% | 42.1% (+15%) | 40.1% | Beyond recent performance |
| Better sales qualified lead to opportunity rate | 36.9% | 42.5% (+15%) | 37.2% | Beyond recent performance |
| Higher average opportunity value | £19,250 | £22,192 (+15%) | £20,000 | Beyond recent performance |

Start with: **more leads in the remaining months**. It needs the smallest stretch that recent months show is possible. Combining two smaller moves is usually easier than one big one.

## Warning signs

- **Marketing qualified to sales qualified** fell from 39.3% to 33.0% (-16%), comparing the first and last 3 months of the window. If it keeps falling, the base forecast is too high.

Find the cause before adding volume: extra leads at a falling conversion rate buy less pipeline than this forecast assumes.

## Assumptions and limits

- Rates are same-month ratios (for example, this month's opportunities divided by this month's sales qualified leads). That works when lead volume is steady; if volume swings sharply, use cohort data.
- Leads created in the last weeks of the quarter often turn into opportunities after it ends, so the final month's contribution is optimistic.
- The forecast is only as good as the stage definitions behind the numbers. If the definition of a qualified lead changed during the window, the rates are not comparable.
